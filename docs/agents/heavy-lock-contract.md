# Heavy-lock supervision ownership contract (#657, phase 1)

This is the required contract for the #657 redesign, not a claim that current `scripts/heavy-lock.sh`, `scripts/tdd/heavy-lock.py`, or the lanes already implement it. It governs a **workload invocation**, including its descendants, and the separate Swift, App, hosted App, and repeat evidence consumers. A test process exiting is not workload cleanup. `PICSEE_HEAVY_LOCK_HELD=1` is a nesting hint, not proof of a held descriptor, a supervisor, or cleanup.

> Specify one authoritative owner for each process group, deadline, cancellation and cleanup result. Nested HELD=1 execution stays inside the parent's cancellation boundary, keeps the acquiring supervisor or guardian FD live, and cannot extend the parent's deadline. CI's HELD=1 without an inherited lock must have an explicit supervisor. Define invocation-scoped status publication without making a nested lane wait for its own ancestor to exit. `complete` attests to verified cleanup, independently of test success; HELD=1 alone proves neither.

## Terms and invariant

- **Invocation**: one command admitted by a supervisor. It has a unique invocation ID, one monotonic execution deadline, one cancellation boundary, and one status record. A lane attempt and every repeat iteration are distinct invocations. An outer invocation can contain nested invocations.
- **Boundary owner**: the one live supervisor accountable for signalling and verifying a process group (and any registered workload outside that group). It may delegate observation of a nested invocation but not its ultimate cancellation duty. A nested supervisor must not create an untracked session/group that the boundary owner cannot cancel.
- **Lock owner**: the shared, inherited kernel flock open-file description, not a PID or the `HELD` flag. The acquiring supervisor retains its own descriptor for the whole lane, including Xcode- or LaunchServices-launched App hosts outside the worker group. A separate guardian retaining a duplicate FD and owning cleanup is required before launch if the supervisor itself may exit before cleanup; it must stay alive through verified host and group drain. Inheriting the FD into cooperative descendants is defence in depth, not the lifetime authority: Xcode/LaunchServices hosts need not inherit it. No participant calls `LOCK_UN`, renames, or unlinks the persistent lock file. The flock remains held while any guardian or inheriting descriptor is open. The mdpro directory is a separate cooperative lock and has its own acquisition/release owner.
- **Cleanup proof**: the boundary owner's verified absence of live workload members after leader reap, plus verified cleanup of any registered out-of-group host and auxiliary supervisor processes. A failed or unavailable process query is *unknown*, not empty. A surviving process after KILL is *incomplete*, even if its RSS is zero. On incomplete cleanup the acquiring owner retains the mdpro directory; the supervisor/guardian keeps its flock FD open while it can continue guarding a survivor. If the guardian itself dies, flock exclusion can no longer be attested (especially with `PICSEE_MDPRO_LOCK=off`): publish no `complete`, return 70 if possible, and do not start dependent work. Never claim that a dead process can retain a flock. No automatic force unlock follows a heuristic.
- **Deadline**: a finite monotonic timestamp set when the command launches, separate from the lock-acquisition wait. Nested effective deadline is `min(parent deadline, requested child deadline)`. It never resets or extends the parent's budget. Proposed #657 execution budgets (not current main defaults) are 5 minutes for discovery, 10 for filtered Swift, 20 for full package, and 30 for coverage/Xcode/asset/unspecified heavy commands; explicit finite per-command overrides are permitted only after preflight validation. Default acquisition wait remains 30 minutes; existing explicit 7200-second lane overrides remain subject to preflight. Proposed normal post-exit drain changes from the current 300 seconds to 30 seconds; phase 2 must update AGENTS.md and the implementation together. At execution timeout, start TERM immediately, then 10 seconds of grace, KILL, and 5 seconds of observation; do not first add normal drain. The outer deadline governs nested cleanup even if an inner grace is longer.

The boundary owner stays outside the group it owns. A normal child exit starts drain verification, not automatic release. Signal cancellation and deadline expiry follow the same bounded escalation. Timeout with verified cleanup exits 124. **Every incomplete, unknown, or unpublished cleanup exits 70, even when the command exited 1, timed out, or received a signal.** Preserve the underlying command exit/signal in the status record and diagnostics only. A configuration or credential refusal before launch also exits 70; lock-acquisition timeout remains 75. The status and command result are separate facts.

## Invocation shapes and ownership transitions

Admission uses two distinct, non-inheritable authorities. The CI workflow supplies a **top-level-only mode input** to its initial supervisor; that supervisor consumes and strips it before spawning any worker. A parent supervisor instead passes a private inherited registration/control FD with its identity, remaining monotonic deadline and cancellation channel to a nested supervisor. The nested supervisor verifies the live parent through this FD and registers its same-session subgroup PGID before launching work. `HELD=1` selects neither mode. With neither valid authority, refuse before launch with 70. A lost inherited flock FD cannot be reclassified as CI top level by adding `HELD=1`. In CI, the parent credential explicitly declares no local flock; with a real local flock it includes a verifiable inherited FD. Each boundary owner keeps a registry of child subgroups and out-of-group hosts, signals each on timeout/cancel, and verifies each before its own `complete`. The child attests only its subgroup and registered hosts, so it can complete while the ancestor stays alive. A subgroup is in the parent's session but has its own PGID; a fresh session outside parent registration is forbidden.

| Shape | Admission and sequence | Deadline and cancellation owner | Lock and status owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Top level** | No `HELD` credential: preflight → acquire flock then mdpro → start guardian and one worker group → drain group and hosts → publish. | Picsee supervisor owns launch-time deadline and signals its group, registered subgroups and hosts. | Acquirer/guardian retains flock FD until verified cleanup; acquirer releases its mdpro directory only after verification. Supervisor publishes local status. |
| **2. Nested `HELD=1` with inherited flock** | Verify parent control FD, live parent and inherited flock FD; register same-session subgroup before launch. Missing or invalid credential/FD exits 70 before launch. | Parent owns outer deadline/cancellation; child owns local subgroup observation and any earlier requested deadline. Parent can signal child subgroup directly, including after child leader reparenting. | Parent/guardian retains authoritative flock FD; child inherits a duplicate where feasible, does not reacquire/unlock, and publishes local status without waiting for parent. |
| **3. CI top-level `HELD=1` without flock** | Only the CI workflow's consumed top-level mode input admits this path. Supervisor records its boundary before launch; worker receives a parent credential with explicit no-flock mode, never the top-level input. | CI supervisor owns deadline/cancellation; job timeout is only a backstop. | No local flock exclusion is claimed. Supervisor publishes status; absent status rejects counts. A nested worker uses shape 5/2's registration rule, never this row. |
| **4. Reverse nesting under mdpro (`MDPRO_HEAVY_LOCK_HELD=1`)** | Verify an mdpro parent control/deadline handoff and direct cancellation reachability **before** trying flock or launching; otherwise exit 70. Flock contention returns 75 without launch. | **Mdpro parent alone owns the outer deadline and cancellation.** Picsee's local deadline is at most the authenticated remaining parent budget; mdpro directly reaches picsee's registered same-session subgroup even if the picsee supervisor exits. Picsee observes local cleanup only. | Mdpro retains its directory; picsee/guardian retains acquired flock through local group and host drain and publishes local status. No picsee action removes mdpro's directory. |
| **5. Hosted repeat** | Controller creates an iteration supervisor and ID. Worker and each inner lane receive only the parent control FD, register their own same-session subgroups and hosted App PIDs, and publish local statuses. | Iteration supervisor owns cancellation of worker, subgroups and registered hosts; controller owns job deadline and iteration ordering. | Supervisor/guardian retains any real flock across the whole iteration, including hosts that cannot inherit it. Controller requires the iteration's own `complete` and valid lane results before the next iteration; after timeout it stops even if cleanup is `complete`. |
| **6. App-lane discovery with HELD cleared** | The existing private-lock discovery path must enter the parent's registry before starting its separate discovery session/group, or be changed to a registered same-session subgroup. Clearing `HELD`, `MDPRO_HELD` or using `PICSEE_MDPRO_LOCK=off` grants no exception. An unregistered discovery launch exits 70. | Containing supervisor owns outer timeout/cancel and directly signals discovery group; discovery supervisor owns local observation with no later deadline. | Discovery's private lock does not replace the outer flock/guardian. Both local discovery status and parent-wide group/host verification are required. |

The registration FD is a capability, not an environment flag: parent passes it only to the intended nested supervisor, confirms registration before admitting the child command, and keeps a control endpoint until its own verification. A supervisor cannot publish parent `complete` while a registered subgroup or host survives or has unknown state. If the local subgroup cannot be reliably identified after reparenting, local status is `incomplete`; the child never waits for the ancestor group to empty.

## Status publication and evidence gate

The supervision channel is separate from child stdout and stderr. Each attempt receives a fresh, cryptographically unpredictable invocation ID generated by its supervisor and a single-use status destination owned by that supervisor. The supervisor conveys the expected ID and destination to its parent consumer over a private control channel, not through child-visible environment or stdout. The child workload must not receive the status destination or authority to write it. Existing CI `PICSEE_APP_RESULT_ID` (`run_id-run_attempt`) and `PICSEE_APP_RESULT_PATH` identify a separate test-result artifact; their predictable ID cannot authenticate cleanup and they cannot replace this channel. The supervisor publishes only after its verification decision, using an atomic one-record write; the consumer binds the record to the expected invocation ID and supervisor identity, rejects a pre-existing/stale record, and reads it only after the publishing supervisor has finished its **local** verification. This ordering never waits for a nested lane's ancestor to exit. The channel must survive or explicitly report publication failure; a log line cannot substitute for it.

| Status value | Attestation | Lane treatment |
|---|---|---|
| `complete` | The named invocation's supervisor verified cleanup of every workload member it owns, including registered out-of-group hosts; the status is independent of whether tests passed or failed. | Only now may a complete, consistent test result count be accepted: pass → 0 with counts; completed assertion failure → 1 with counts and potentially a valid RED receipt. Timeout/cancellation never supplies assertion evidence even if cleanup completed. |
| `incomplete` | A survivor was seen, ownership was lost, verification failed/was inconclusive, or cleanup could not be completed. It never means test failure alone. | 70 without counts or valid RED. Preserve underlying exit/signal and survivor diagnostics separately. No next hosted repeat iteration. |
| `missing`, malformed, stale, contradictory, or unpublished | No trustworthy attestation for this invocation. These are consumer classifications, not positive supervisor assertions. | 70 without counts or valid RED. Do not infer `complete` from exit 0/1, output summaries, `HELD=1`, or stderr. |

A supervisor may publish `complete` after successfully cleaning up a timed-out or cancelled invocation, but the execution outcome remains timeout/cancel and the lane remains invalid for test counts/RED. A separate publication failure cannot be converted into `complete` and produces exit 70 without counts. Every retry gets a new ID and status destination; one attempt's record cannot bless another. The same gate applies to Swift, App, hosted App and combined/repeat summaries. A combined lane reports counts only if every component it includes has a valid local attestation and complete result. A repeat controller reports a later iteration only after the prior iteration's own group/host cleanup is verified.

## Open assumptions and phase-2 fail-closed defaults

- Current code has no private inherited control/registration FD or authenticated parent deadline. **Default:** `HELD=1` with no verified parent credential and no consumed workflow top-level input exits 70 before launch; with a claimed inherited flock but missing FD, also 70. Phase 2 must provide the capability rather than infer it from flags.
- Current CI uses `HELD=1` without local flock. Its job is treated as single-tenant for local admission, without a cross-job exclusion claim. **Default:** absent workflow top-level mode input or explicit supervisor exits 70 before launch.
- Current `app-repeat-process.mjs` `drained` JSON is not authenticated invocation status. **Default:** classify iteration status `missing`, exit 70 and stop before the next iteration until adapted or replaced.
- Current nested code has no reliable local subgroup identity across reparenting. **Default:** refuse nested launch with 70 until parent registration and cancellation reachability are verified; if identity is lost after launch, report `incomplete` and exit 70.
- Current mdpro nesting has no authenticated outer deadline/cancellation handoff. **Default:** reverse nesting exits 70 before flock acquisition or child launch until mdpro can pass a verified deadline and direct registered-subgroup cancellation capability. A separately enforced backstop is insufficient.
- Current HELD-cleared App discovery starts an unregistered fresh session. **Default:** nested discovery exits 70 before launch until its group is registered with the containing owner and reachable on timeout/cancel.
- Current code cannot guarantee flock retention if its only holder dies, particularly with mdpro disabled. **Default:** require a live guardian retaining the FD before launch where supervisor loss could leave work alive; inability to establish one exits 70 before launch. If both holders are lost later, status is missing/incomplete, dependent work stops with 70, and no exclusion claim is made.
