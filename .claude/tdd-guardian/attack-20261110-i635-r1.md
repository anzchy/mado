- **Attack**: Wrong-but-close boundary / order-dependent.
- **Passing-but-wrong implementation**:
  ```text
  await original lifecycle predicate
  await Task.yield()

  ```
- **Why every listed case passes**: An immediately queued count release may finish during that yield. Ordinary delivery and repetitions can also pass. The matrix did not explicitly require that readiness remains false while independently held count delivery is still pending.

Natural RED may still establish the original failure, but the correction must use the actual count rather than a scheduling guess.
